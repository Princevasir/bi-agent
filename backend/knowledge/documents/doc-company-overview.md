# Trailhead Outfitters: company overview

## Who we are
Trailhead Outfitters sells outdoor gear for camping, hiking, climbing and everyday adventure. Customers buy from our website and in our own stores. The business is organised in three sales regions: North America, Europe and Asia Pacific.

## Where we sell
We serve customers in eight countries: the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Germany, France, the Netherlands, Australia and New Zealand.

## How orders reach customers
Online orders ship from four distribution centres: Reno DC (United States), Toronto DC (Canada), Rotterdam DC (United Kingdom and the rest of Europe) and Sydney DC (Australia and New Zealand).

## What we sell
The range is organised in five top-level categories: Camping, Hiking, Apparel, Climbing and Accessories. Our own private label is Trailhead Essentials; we also sell Ridgeline, Alpenglow, Cascade Works, Granite Peak, Wildpath, Driftwood and Kestrel.

## How the year works
December is our biggest month. Camping gear peaks in summer (June to August) and insulated apparel in winter. Our fiscal year starts on 1 February (see the fiscal calendar rule).

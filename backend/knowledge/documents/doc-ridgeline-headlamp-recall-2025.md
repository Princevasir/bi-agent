# Ridgeline 300 Headlamp recall (2025)

On 10 June 2025 the Ridgeline 300 Headlamp was recalled for a battery fault and taken off sale (`products.discontinued_on`). Customers who had bought it since January 2025 were asked to return it; those returns carry reason `recall` in `returns`.

When reporting return rates for Lighting or for 2025 as a whole, show figures with and without recall returns.

# Store operations handbook

## Store types
- **Flagship** stores (Seattle, London, Melbourne) carry the full range, host events and are the largest.
- **Standard** stores carry the core range.
- **Outlet** (Reno Outlet) sells end-of-line and discounted stock; compare it with other outlets only.

## Staffing
Every store has one Store Manager, who reports to the Head of Store Operations, and four to nine Sales Associates who report to the Store Manager. New stores hire their team about two months before opening.

## Payments in stores
Store customers pay by card or cash. Loyalty members are identified at the till so that their tier discount applies and the order is linked to their account; about half of store orders are from unidentified (guest) customers.

## Store performance
- Compare stores with **revenue per square metre** (see the metric) rather than total revenue, because floor areas range from 850 to 2,400 square metres.
- Compare like for like: only stores open for the whole period (see the rule *Store comparisons*). Boulder Basecamp opened on 1 July 2025.
- Store revenue follows the same seasons as online, with December the strongest month.

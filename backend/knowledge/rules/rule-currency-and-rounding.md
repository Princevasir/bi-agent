# Currency and rounding

All money is in **US dollars**, including sales in Europe and Asia Pacific (converted at the daily rate when the order was placed). Store amounts with two decimals; round only for display: totals to whole dollars, averages and per-unit figures to two decimals, rates and shares to one decimal place as percentages.

# Spring Trail Sale 2025: review

**What ran.** Code **SPRINGTRAIL25** gave 20% off across online and stores from 1 to 30 April 2025.

**Result.** April 2025 revenue was well above April 2024: more orders came in, and many of them used the code, which more than offset the discount. The code appears on orders in `order_promotions`.

**Caveat.** Comparisons of April 2026 with April 2025 should mention the sale, otherwise April 2026 looks weak.

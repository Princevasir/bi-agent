Customer orders, online and in stores, January 2024 to September 2026.

Grain: One row per order. Primary key: order_id

Referenced by: order_items.order_id, order_promotions.order_id, payments.order_id, shipments.order_id, support_tickets.order_id, web_sessions.order_id

Watch out:

  • Order totals are already summed from their items. Never SUM(orders.total_amount) after joining order_items: each order would be counted once per item.
  • Guest checkouts have an empty customer_id; an inner join to customers drops them. Use a LEFT JOIN when counting all orders.

  • Returns are not subtracted here; they are in returns, per item.

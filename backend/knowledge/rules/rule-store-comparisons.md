# Store comparisons

Compare stores **like for like**: only include stores that were open for the whole of every period being compared. Boulder Basecamp opened on 1 July 2025, so it is left out of comparisons of 2025 with 2024 and included from July 2026 onwards. Name any store that was excluded.

# Quarterly business review: Q2 2026

## Headline
Q2 2026 was a recovery quarter. After the tent supply disruption in February and March, camping stock was back in all warehouses from April, and May and June were strong ahead of the summer season.

## Sales
Online kept growing faster than stores. Boulder Basecamp approached its first anniversary and is now compared like for like from July 2026.

## Supply chain
The backup supplier for tents was used for the first time. Stock-out weeks for camping categories fell to normal levels.

## Customers
Loyalty upgrades continued; Gold and Platinum members remain the most valuable customers.

## Focus for Q3
Summer camping season, the Summer Camp Sale in July, and preparing stock for Black Friday and December.

*Figures are in the Executive Sales dashboard; this review gives the context.*

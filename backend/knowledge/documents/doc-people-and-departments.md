# People and departments

The company has seven departments: Executive, Store Operations, E-commerce, Supply Chain, Marketing, Customer Service and Finance. The Chief Executive Officer leads the Executive department; each other department has a Head who reports to the CEO. Store staff belong to Store Operations: Store Managers report to its Head, and Sales Associates to their Store Manager.

Employee names, work email addresses and salaries are personal data and are not available to analysts. Headcount, job titles, departments, stores and hire dates are available.

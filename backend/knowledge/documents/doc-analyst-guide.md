# How to ask the BI agent good questions

## Say what, when and how
- **What:** name the measure (revenue, orders, units, return rate).
- **When:** give the period ("in 2025", "in September 2026", "last month"). Without one, the agent may use all data.
- **How:** say the breakdown ("by region", "by month", "by category").

## Calendar or fiscal
"2025" means the calendar year. Say "fiscal 2025" or "FY2025" for 1 February 2025 to 31 January 2026.

## Charts
Ask for a chart by name ("as a pie chart", "as a line chart"), or say "make it a donut" after an answer to redraw it without a new query.

## About the data
"What tables do I have access to?", "Describe the orders table" and "What does line_total mean?" are answered from the data catalog.

## What you will not get
Personal data (names, email addresses, phone numbers, street addresses, salaries) is not available to analysts. Data ends on 30 September 2026.

# Merchandising calendar

## Seasons
- **Winter (November to February):** insulated jackets and base layers sell most; camping is quiet.
- **Spring (March to May):** camping and hiking ramp up; the spring sale (when one runs) lands in April.
- **Summer (June to August):** peak camping season for tents, sleeping bags and camp kitchen.
- **Autumn (September and October):** steady; range changes and price updates.

## Peak trading
December is the biggest month of the year, driven by gifting and Black Friday at the end of November. Late November and December need the most stock and staff.

## Planning implications
Camping stock must be in the warehouses by February. A supply problem in early spring hurts more than one in autumn (see the Q1 2026 tent supply incident).
